# Katsuk Peak (8,680 ft, Bulger #36)

## Personal status
- **Climbed:** no
- **Style preference:** TBD. Best done as part of the **full Ragged Ridge Traverse** with Mesahchie, Kimtah, and Cosho — see [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md), [kimtah.md](./kimtah.md), [cosho.md](./cosho.md). The four peaks share approach, camp, and the 7,400-ft south-side traverse line; doing one in isolation gives up the natural efficiency.

## Summary
Second peak from the east on Ragged Ridge, directly west of Mesahchie. PeakBagger lidar: **8,683 ft** (officially named, dGPS-confirmed east summit slightly higher than west). Prominence per LIDAR is **436 ft** (key col = Katsuk-Mesahchie col 8,247 ft) — note the JSON value of 80 ft is significantly low and reflects legacy contour-based data. Line parent: Mesahchie Peak. Coords 48.5784 N, 120.8889 W. PeakBagger PID 1823.

Compact summit block with a distinctive "mushroom" appearance from the south. The summit pitch is **a 50-ft Class 4 block climbed on its west side**, perched above a steep gully. Of the four Ragged Ridge Bulgers, Katsuk has the smallest prominence and is the most clearly "linked" to its neighbor (Mesahchie) — but the summit gully and block are non-trivial. **Region 6: Ragged Ridge.** North Cascades NP, Stephen Mather Wilderness, Skagit County.

## Best-season window
- **Late July through mid-September** — prime. Dry rock, manageable snow.
- **June – mid-July:** more snow in the summit gully, including a "scary runout" snowfield just east of the standard line — early-season ascent commits to steep snow above cliffs.
- **Late spring (ski):** north-side Katsuk Glacier is a popular ski objective from the spring traverse; the summit block itself is **not** a ski summit.
- **October:** in until first significant snow.

## Access — Easy Pass TH (SR 20)
- Same as [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md). SR 20 closure season Nov–late May (Easy Pass TH east of closure). Trail to Easy Pass 3.6 mi / 2,800 ft.

## Standard route — South Face (Class 3–4 with Class 4 summit block)
Lemke 2014 is the modern definitive TR; this matches Beckey's South Face description (also Lemke-cataloged as "Class 3").

### Approach
Same as Mesahchie up to the 6,600-ft basin camp south of Mesahchie:
1. Easy Pass TH → Easy Pass (3.6 mi).
2. Leave trail at pass, sidehill traverse west on 6,600–6,800 ft contour ~1 mi.
3. Camp at 6,600 ft basin south of Mesahchie. **From this camp, Katsuk is a 2-hour summit RT** (Lemke).

### Summit pitch
1. **From the 6,600-ft camp basin, re-ascend the Class 4 ribs to the 7,400-ft bench** (the bench south of Mesahchie/Katsuk; same one used for the Kimtah/Cosho traverse).
2. **Short ascending traverse west** to a **small waterfall** at the entrance to the standard summit gully.
3. **Climb left of the water** to avoid the waterfall.
4. **Blocky mix of granite and slate** on either side of the flowing water — granite quality is much better than slate.
5. **At ~8,100 ft the gully forks** — take the **left fork**, which leads directly to the summit block.
6. **Avoid the steeper summit snowfield** (scary runout) by climbing the **Class 3–4 rib just left of it**, separating it from another scary-looking gully further south and west.
7. **Final 200 ft to the summit block:** follow the small moat on the left side of the snowfield as the rib narrows and gets harder.
8. **50-ft summit block: Class 4, climbed on the west side.** Plastic-pill-container summit register placed 2004.

### Descent
Reverse the route — same gully, same rib, back down the 4th-class ribs to camp.

### Stats (Katsuk only, from Easy Pass TH)
- ~12 mi RT, ~5,800 ft gain. Day-trip feasible but a long day given the approach traverse.
- **From camp:** ~2 h to summit, ~30 min on top, ~1.5 h back = ~4-h summit RT.

## Linkup with Mesahchie
Mesahchie's standard summit route starts from the **same 7,400-ft bench**, just further east. **A Mesahchie + Katsuk day from camp is the natural pairing** — both summits ~2 h apart on the same line. Lemke and other parties typically do them on adjacent days if doing all four; speed parties hit them in sequence in the morning before turning west to Kimtah/Cosho.

## Equipment
- **Helmet** — debris in the summit gully.
- Approach shoes adequate; sticky-rubber boot or light alpine boot is better on the summit block.
- **30-m rope + ~3 small cams** valuable for a belay/rappel on the summit block descent. Many parties solo it.
- Ice axe + light crampons useful for the snowfield at the gully fork through August.

## Ski variant
Katsuk Glacier (north side) is part of the standard 3-glacier spring ski traverse (Mesahchie → Katsuk → Kimtah Glaciers, exiting back to SR 20 via Mesahchie Pass / Granite Creek). **Summit block not skiable**; the glacier itself is the objective. See [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md) ski variant section for Galen Weld 2025 trip beta.

## Hazards
- **Steep snowfield with scary runout** at the gully fork — funnels onto talus/slabs. Class 3–4 rib variant left of it is the safe option.
- **Loose rock and rockfall potential** in the summit gully — single party of 2–3 strongly preferred.
- **Crux summit block: Class 4 on the west side** — most committing single section. Rope-up is reasonable, especially in wet conditions or if combining with the rest of the ridge while tired.
