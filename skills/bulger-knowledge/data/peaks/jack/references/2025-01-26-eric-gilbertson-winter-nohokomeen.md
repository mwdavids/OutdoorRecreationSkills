# Eric Gilbertson — Jack Mountain Winter Ascent (Nohokomeen Headwall), 2025-01-26

← Peak file: [jack.md](../../jack.md)

**Source:** [countryhighpoints.com/jack-mountain-winter-ascent/](https://www.countryhighpoints.com/jack-mountain-winter-ascent/)
**Author:** Eric Gilbertson + Ryan Stoddard
**Date:** January 25–26, 2025 (climbed Jan 26)
**Route:** Nohokomeen Headwall (north side), winter conditions
**Style:** 2-day overnight, glacier bivy at 7,800 ft. **25 mi hike/ski + 8 mi biking.** Second winter ascent of Jack Mountain.

## Why this is preserved in full
- **Modern access details from the Ross Dam TH side**, including the bike-down-SR-20 trick when the road closure puts the gate up-valley.
- Documents a viable **glacier-bivy strategy at 7,800 ft** on the Nohokomeen — useful for the 2-day variant of a spring ascent.
- Captures the **descent crevasse-avoidance line** (skiers' right of glacier) — different from ascent line.
- Confirms the bushwhack route through the cliff band at ~4,000 ft using the same line as the 2018 trip — same rap-anchor tree confirms standard route.
- Demonstrates that **Nohokomeen is climbable in winter when snow is stable**, with a cliff band poking through midway and a single narrow snow/ice finger crossing it directly under the summit. (Lean / mid-winter snow conditions look very different from spring.)

## Day-by-day
- **Fri night Jan 24:** Slept at Ross Dam TH parking.
- **Sat Jan 25:** Moving by **4:30am**.
  - **Bike SR 20 from MP 134 closure to East Bank TH spur**: ~4 mi. First mile slightly uphill, remaining 3 mi downhill = quick. Frosty road; mountain bikes for traction. Cold pre-dawn → mittens + down jacket.
  - **East Bank TH at ~5:00am**, locked bikes.
  - Hiked snow-free East Bank Trail ~8 mi to **May Creek by 8:00am** just after sunrise (in approach shoes / trail runners — only minor blowdowns).
  - **Bushwhack S of May Creek**: same route as Eric 2018. Open forest. Found the small cliff band at ~4,000 ft and the **same rap-anchor tree** above it → confirmed standard route.
  - Just above cliffs the snow started → switched from approach shoes to **ski boots + crampons.** At ~4,500 ft snow deep enough to skin → skis on.
  - **Crested ridge at 5,000 ft** with first views of summit. **Lower glacier was very icy and cracked up on the right side.** Headwall looked thin — lots of rocks poking out. But a viable snow line was visible to the summit.
  - **Traversed east at ~5,000 ft** to gain the Nohokomeen Glacier basin (same as 2018).
  - Skinned up aiming for the **left side of the glacier** (same as 2018). Cold + wind squalls almost made them bivy in trees but the glacier view won out.
  - Passed through an "interesting half-pipe feature" before reaching the upper glacier. Roped up.
  - Upper glacier: angled toward middle to avoid steep left side. Got partially blocked by crevasses but found a way through.
  - **Glacier bivy at 7,800 ft by 4:30pm.** Mega-mid tent. Sunset over Pickets / Ross Lake.
- **Sun Jan 26:** Slept in (forecast lows in single digits). Left camp at **7:15am.**
  - Skinned up to base of headwall. **Unlike 2018**, this time a **cliff band spanned nearly the entire face near the bottom**, with **one narrow snow/ice finger directly beneath the summit** = the only line. Aimed for that.
  - **Ditched skis just before the steep wall**, switched to crampons.
  - Eric led with **rack: 4 pickets, cams, nuts, hexes, ice screws.** Each climber: one technical tool + one straight-shaft for plunging.
  - Climbed up through the narrow icy gap in the cliff band, then **traversed climbers' right** to just below the low point in the ridge, then directly up to the ridge.
  - Rock outcrops between pitches → could place rock gear most of the way + occasional pickets.
  - **3 hours to the ridge** → out of gear → built ice-axe anchor in flat windless spot, belayed Ryan up.
  - Ryan led one final simul-pitch up to the **summit by 10:45am.**
  - Summit panorama: Redoubt + Easy Mox visible (Eric had been there a week earlier). Mountain goat tracks on SE ridge nearly to summit.
- **Descent:**
  - **Simul-downclimbed back to ridge low-point**, then downclimbed the route placing the same gear in reverse. (60m rope = many rappels otherwise = lost gear.)
  - **Back at base of route by 12:30pm**, ski to camp, packed up.
  - **Skied out hugging skiers' right side of the glacier** to avoid the ascent-line crevasse field. Wind-affected snow up high; pockets of powder lower. Half-pipe was fun.
  - Transitioned to crampons at ~4,500 ft in the woods, **rapped down the cliff band** at ~4,000 ft (used a fixed anchor).
  - Hiked out to East Bank Trail by sunset, biked back to Ross Dam TH parking by **9:30pm**.

## Jack-specific takeaways (winter / lean conditions)
1. **Winter Nohokomeen is feasible in stable cold-snowpack windows** but the route looks very different from spring: a **cliff band spans most of the lower headwall**, leaving a single snow/ice finger as the only line. Verify visually before committing — boat from Ross Lake (or pre-trip view from a previous trip) is how Eric scouted.
2. **Lower glacier is very crevassed/icy on the right side in winter** — traverse around to the left/center for ascent. **Descent: hug skiers' right** to bypass the worst of the ascent-line crevasse field.
3. **Glacier bivy at 7,800 ft works** — flat, dramatic view, sets up a reasonable summit day. This is also a valid strategy for the spring 2-day variant.
4. **Cliff band at ~4,000 ft has a fixed rap anchor** above the steep step — confirms standard route both directions. Useful in the dark on descent.
5. **Bike-down-SR-20 access** when the road closure is at MP 134: easy 4 mi, first mile uphill outbound, fast downhill return. Mountain bikes preferred for frosty pavement.
6. **Mountain goats use the SE ridge** to near the summit (tracks observed) — confirms the South Face / SE ridge is genuinely walkable when snow-free.
7. **Snow-free East Bank Trail in mid-winter** in lean / no-snow seasons. Don't assume early winter requires skis on the trail — check current conditions.

## Access takeaways
- **Ross Dam TH parking → bike SR 20 4 mi → East Bank TH → hike East Bank Trail 8 mi → May Creek → bushwhack → glacier.** Same approach as 2018 plus the bike segment when the SR 20 closure is in effect.
- Round trip from car (winter, with bivy at 7,800 ft): **car at 4:30am Sat → camp at 4:30pm Sat → summit at 10:45am Sun → car at 9:30pm Sun.** ≈ 41 hr car-to-car.
- **Ross Lake Resort water taxi does NOT run in winter** (ruled out).
- Eric's personal Zodiac boat is the alternative water-approach in winter (3+ hrs to triple-carry & drag to Ross Lake + 1 hr to motor to May Creek = ~comparable to bike approach).

## Verbatim key quotes
- On the headwall in lean conditions: "Unlike in June 2018, when the face was mostly snow, this time there was a cliff band near the bottom that spanned nearly the entire face. There was luckily one narrow snow/ice finger crossing the cliff band, located directly beneath the summit."
- On gear strategy: "I led the way up with a solid rack of four pickets, cams, nuts, hexes, and screws. One way or another I was determined to find pro on the climb."
- On descent strategy: "It made most sense to just downclimb the route, since our 60m rope would require many rappels and leaving lots of gear."
