# Jack Mountain — East Ridge (up) + South Face (down), Sept 13, 2008 (JasonG)

**Source:** https://cascadeclimbers.com/forum/topic/62434-tr-jack-eastridge-9132008/
**Author:** JasonG (cascadeclimbers.com moderator)
**Party:** JasonG, Tim, T.J. (party of 3)
**Date:** September 13, 2008 (~48 hr car-to-car, spread over 3 days)
**Style:** Up the East Ridge, **down the South Face** — full traverse of the mountain.

## Why preserved
- **Older companion** to the [JDT 2025 East Ridge cache](2025-07-11-jdt-damon-jon-east-ridge.md). Same route, very different conditions (mid-Sept vs mid-July, late-season broken Jerry Glacier vs intact mid-summer snowfield).
- **Documents the East-up-South-down traverse** as a real option — only TR in our cache that does both routes in one trip.
- **Late-season Jerry Glacier data point:** "pretty broken" in mid-September 2008. Forced the party off snow and into wet/loose 4th class to gain the ridge — confirms that East Ridge approach difficulty is **strongly snow-dependent**.
- Brief — just the highlights.

## Trip overview (3 days, ~48 hrs c2c)
- **Day 1:** Canyon Creek TH → trail to Crater Lake → cross-country around Crater Mountain past Jerry Glacier → Jerry Lakes camp. (Per the author: "fast on trail" up to Crater Lake.)
- **Day 2 (summit day, East Ridge up / South Face down):**
  - Climbed above Jerry Lakes for view of route ("right skyline").
  - Dropped "a disheartening amount" on the other side (the same 800-ft drop into the adjacent basin documented by JDT 2025).
  - Climb to east glacier. **Glacier was pretty broken** — forced wet, loose 4th class scrambling to gain the ridge instead of the straightforward snow travel that would be standard with the glacier intact.
  - On the ridge: **"surprisingly good" 3rd and 4th class, with a snow bit towards the top** — matches Beckey's description.
  - **Belayed a short bit right before the summit**; rest carefully scrambled.
  - **Time:** ~3 hrs to gain the ridge, ~2 hrs climbing the ridge, plus summit hangout time delaying the "reportedly unsavory descent."
- **Descent (South Face):** "long and tedious where you have to stay on your toes." **"Never very serious"** but slow, especially without prior up-knowledge of the line. "Slow going."
- **Day 3:** out.

## Key beta vs the JDT 2025 East Ridge cache
| Element | JasonG 2008-09-13 (this) | JDT 2025-07-11 |
|---|---|---|
| Season | mid-September | mid-July |
| Jerry Glacier | "pretty broken" | snowfield intact |
| Approach to ridge | wet, loose 4th class scrambling (forced off snow) | snowfield + scrambling, but snow finger collapsed at glacier toe |
| 5th-class pitches | Author says "pretty much just carefully scrambled... belayed a short bit right before the summit" | "3 distinct 5th-class pitches" including a "wildly exposed 5.0" mid-ridge crux |
| Descent | South Face (full traverse) | reverse East Ridge with one rappel |
| Summit-day time | ~3 hr to ridge + ~2 hr climbing + summit hangout = ~5 hr up | 13 h 08 m round-trip Day 2 |

**The two TRs disagree on technical difficulty.** JasonG's party found mostly carefully-scrambled 3rd/4th with one short belayed pitch near the summit; JDT's party found 3 full 5th-class pitches with a 5.0 crux. Possible explanations:
- Slight route variation on the ridge (where exactly you traverse onto it / how high you climb on the snowfield first).
- Different protection standards / belay decisions per party.
- Snow conditions changing where rock is exposed or covered.

For a future party: assume JDT's harder version is the realistic worst case — bring gear and partners for **3 pitches of 5th** even if you may only end up belaying one short bit.

## Practical takeaways
- **Late-season East Ridge** (mid-Sept and later) loses the snowfield approach to the ridge → expect wet, loose, dangerous 4th class to get on the ridge proper. The ridge climbing itself is unchanged.
- **Full traverse (East Ridge up, South Face down)** is feasible in 3 days car-to-car. Trade-off: you skip the East Ridge downclimb and rappel, but the South Face descent is long, tedious, and route-finding-intensive without prior up-knowledge.
- **Combine with a Crater Mountain tag-on (JDT-style, Day 3)** is plausible since the approach already passes Crater Lake.
