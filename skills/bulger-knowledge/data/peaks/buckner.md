# Mount Buckner (9,114 ft, Bulger #11)

## Personal status
- **Climbed:** no
- **Attempts:** 1 — most recent [2026-05-04 — turned at Sahale-Boston Col](buckner/2026-05-04-attempt.md)
- **Style preference:** ski mountaineering, N Face

## Summary
Big N Cascades alpine objective in the Cascade Pass / Boston Basin area. Spring ski mountaineering of the N Face is the prize line, but the *approach* — specifically gaining the Boston Glacier — is the real difficulty in May/early June, not the face itself.

## Best-season window
- **Spring N Face ski (mid-April to mid-May):** good face conditions, but gated by whichever col approach is in shape that year
- **Summer N Face / Park Creek Pass (July–August):** col traverses melt out, becomes a more conventional alpine outing
- The JSON's `best_seasons: ["spring"]` is correct in spirit but underspecified — the limiter is the Boston Glacier approach

## Standard route — N Face via Sahale-Boston Col
Eldorado TH → Cascade River Rd gate (currently closed at MP 20 per NPS 9/30/2025) → bike/walk to Cascade Pass TH → Sahale Arm → Sahale-Boston Col → traverse onto upper Boston Glacier → Buckner N Face → reverse.

The textbook spring approach. **The col traverse is a real spring crux** — see Crux/hazards below; verified impassable on [2026-05-04](buckner/2026-05-04-attempt.md).

## Alternate route — N Face via Sharkfin Col (Boston Basin approach)
Eldorado TH → Boston Basin Trail → Boston Basin → Sharkfin Col (right-side snow couloir, rappel notch) → Boston Glacier east → Buckner N Face → reverse via Sharkfin Col.

- **When to use:** when the Sahale-Boston Col is loaded with impassable steep snow (typical late-April through mid-June many years)
- **Penalty:** ~3–4 mi extra one-way; realistically a 2-day trip with bivy in Boston Basin (a fast strong party could do c2c in 16–18+ hrs)
- **Best window:** mid-April to mid-May, possibly into early June. Boston Basin Trail melts out and rockfall in the col approach gully starts late May / early June.
- **Permit:** Boston Basin cross-country zone permit (NOCA) required for any bivy on Torment-Forbidden-Boston-Sahale ridgeline or on Boston/Forbidden Glaciers
- Detailed third-party beta on the Sharkfin Col approach: [Climber Kyle Forbidden Tour, 2024-03-30](buckner/references/2024-03-30-climber-kyle-forbidden-tour.md)

## Alternate route — Park Creek Pass (Stehekin side)
Long, avoids both cols. Useful when standard spring approaches are blown out, or for a summer ascent. Logistics from Stehekin (drive, ferry, distance to base) still need researching — see Open questions.

## Access & logistics
- **Trailheads:** Eldorado TH (Sahale-Boston Col approach) or Boston Basin TH at MP 22 (Sharkfin Col approach)
- **Cascade River Rd:** Closed at MP 20 (Eldorado TH) as of 9/30/2025 per NPS alert; ~2 mi walk/bike to Boston Basin TH
- **Drive from North Bend:** ~3.5 hrs to Marblemount, then ~45 min up Cascade River Rd
- **Permits:** NOCA wilderness permit; Boston Basin cross-country zone permit if bivying
- **SNOTEL:** Thunder Basin (`817:WA:SNTL`, 4,310 ft) — JSON has this correct

## Crux / hazards

**Sahale-Boston Col → Boston Glacier traverse** is the primary objective hazard in May/early June, *not* the N Face. Extremely steep side-hill snow traverse to drop onto the upper Boston Glacier; verified impassable on [2026-05-04](buckner/2026-05-04-attempt.md). Public TRs from summer/fall (bare rock or low-angle firn) do not represent the spring problem — discount them. Typical melt-out unknown, likely late June / early July (open question). Pre-trip filter: ski-cramponing/booting capability on 50°+ side-hill snow.

**Sharkfin Col rappel & Boston Glacier:** right-side snow couloir to a notch ~20–30 ft above the col, fixed slings; 2× 30m or one 60m for clean rappel. Boston Glacier is large and crevassed — well-covered Apr–May, opens up in June; rope team mandatory. Small cornice on col may need to be broken through to start rappel.

**N Face itself:** steep snow ski descent, generally less of a problem than the access cols in spring. The face is the reward, not the limiter.
