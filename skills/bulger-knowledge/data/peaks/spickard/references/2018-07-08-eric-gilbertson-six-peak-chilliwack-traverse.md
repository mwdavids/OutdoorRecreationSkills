# Eric Gilbertson + Steven Song — Easy Mox, Hard Mox, Redoubt, Spickard, Rahm, and Custer (six-peak Chilliwack traverse)

**Source:** https://www.countryhighpoints.com/easy-mox-hard-mox-redoubt-spickard-rahm-and-custer/
**Author:** Eric Gilbertson
**Posted:** 2018-07-12
**Trip dates:** 2018-07-07 through 2018-07-11
**Party:** Eric Gilbertson, Steven Song
**Outcome:** All six Chilliwack Bulgers in one push.

## Why preserved in full
Canonical summer beta for the Depot Creek approach + south-face route on Spickard. Almost every modern Spickard ascent uses some version of this approach; Eric's writeup is one of the most detailed in print and the author is highly reliable. Also captures the multi-peak Chilliwack traverse strategy for future trip planning.

## Day-by-day summary

### July 7 — Approach
- Eric bussed to Sedro Woolley, met Steven, **picked up NoCa NP back-country permit** (rangers do patrol; many groups skip the permit and risk being ejected)
- Drove into BC, dinner at Steven's in Vancouver, switched to a Toyota Tacoma
- Drove to Chilliwack Lake. At south end of lake, **turned left at intersection where several Subarus were parked** — road got rough. Tacoma handled it. Stopped at first wide spot where branches hung low enough to block all but ATVs. Slept in truck.

### July 8 — Easy Mox + Redoubt
- Up at 3am, hiking the road. Turned left at a cairn, ascended a washed-out streambed. Eventually intersected an old logging road with fresh ATV tracks; turned right and followed it up the old road grade. Reached the **cleared US-Canada border swath before dawn**.
- Signed in at the **NoCa NP kiosk on the US side**, proceeded on unmaintained climbers' trail up Depot Creek (hard to follow in dark).
- After sunrise: trail reaches a **big waterfall with a fixed rope** to help scramble up steep, slippery rocks. Steep ascent in trees beside the waterfall, then a flat plateau.
- At edge of trees, Eric switched to Evo Nepals (rock shoes); hung trail runners in a tree.
- **Shortcut for an ambitious itinerary:** instead of going to Lake Ouzel basecamp, ascended scree + slabs **directly up to the Redoubt Glacier** from the edge of treeline.
- Climbed Easy Mox (NE ridge from saddle below pt 7647 — slab section then 5th-class step to ridge crest, then class 3+ to summit; rappel descent).
- Returned to glacier, traversed unroped to stashed gear, ascended to 7,400 ft col at south end of Redoubt Glacier and dropped overnight gear at a flat campsite on rocks.
- **Climbed Redoubt** that afternoon (snow then rocky 3rd-class gully, snow-filled chockstone notch, short summit pitch). Rappelled, returned to camp.

### July 9 — Hard Mox + storm
- Traversed and ascended scree to **Col of the Wild**. Right and up via cairns to **Ridge of Gendarmes**, crossed to south side.
- Traversed east on cairns, with one **brief 10-ft narrow exposed section**.
- Rappelled into the head of a snow gully (30 m rope to end + downclimbing), traversed to the next gully — this is the **route-finding crux of the climb**.
- Roped 4th-class on right side of right gully past a chockstone, then 3rd class up to the ridge. Pedestal anchor with many rap cords. Steven led continuation up + slightly left to a second rap anchor; unroped class 3-4 to summit. Summit register had a **photocopy of the Fred Beckey 1941 first-ascent log**.
- Returned to camp, watched storm clouds engulf Challenger and Fury, decided to wait out the predicted bad weather. Hard rain through the night.

### July 10 — Move camp to Lake Ouzel + Spickard + Rahm
- Slept in. Stopped raining by 8am but socked in. Packed up, descended the Redoubt Glacier to **Lake Ouzel** (below clouds). Met a 7-person party arriving for the same area.
- Set up camp at Lake Ouzel; cached extra food on a boulder.
- **Climbed Spickard** in low visibility, **following the previous day's group's tracks**:
  - Ascended to **just below the col leading to Silver Lake**, then cut to the right.
  - Climbed **steep snow slopes to the south ridge of Spickard**.
  - Crossed **a small notch to gain the east-face snowfield**.
  - Traversed snowfield a few hundred feet, then up a gully back to the south ridge.
  - Roughly followed the ridge, **traversing below several false summits**.
  - Scrambled to the true summit in early afternoon. Whiteout, no view.
- Descended same route to col leading to Silver Lake. Decided there was time for **Rahm Peak**:
  - From the col, scrambled up rocky face to gain a large bench.
  - Hiked NE on bench (some snow traversing), occasionally seeing Silver Lake through clouds.
  - Reached three gullies dropping from Rahm; aimed for the **lowest gully (3rd class)**.
  - **Au-cheval snow ridge** to gain the gully base, then loose 3rd-class scramble to the SW ridge of Rahm. Climbers' trail to summit.
- Considered the W ridge over to Custer (on the way back) but turned around due to exposure beta from prior reports + late hour. Saved Custer for next day.
- Back to camp by sunset; skies clearing.

### July 11 — Custer + exit
- Traversed left around Pt 8205 to gain the basin SW of Custer.
- Ascended snow as high as possible, then onto **southwest scree slopes** (per Jake Robinson's beta — avoid the south ridge, which has a "narrow, exposed, loose section" that has turned groups around).
- "Choss" (loose rock); Steven led route-finding through the screes. Onto W ridge crest, traversed right to summit.
- Descended same route, scree-surfed lower portion.
- Hiked out to truck by mid-afternoon. Drove to Bellingham; Katie picked Eric up there.

## Key Spickard-specific takeaways

- **South face from Lake Ouzel is the standard summer route** — verified line: col-toward-Silver-Lake (don't go to it) → cut right → steep snow → S ridge → cross small notch → E face snowfield → gully → back to S ridge → traverse below false summits → scramble to true summit.
- **Climbing-grade is moderate** (steep snow + class 3-4) — no technical pitches. Spickard is the easiest of the six Chilliwack Bulgers in this area.
- **Route-finding among false summits** is real in poor visibility — following another party's tracks helped Eric in a whiteout.
- **Half-day round trip from Lake Ouzel basecamp** is realistic in good conditions (Eric also tagged Rahm the same afternoon).

## Key approach takeaways

- **Permit:** pick up at Sedro Woolley. Don't skip — rangers patrol despite the remoteness.
- **Driving:** Tacoma minimum for the southerly logging road extension off Chilliwack Lake Road. Subarus park at the rough intersection at the south end of the lake.
- **Border crossing:** legal sign-in at NoCa kiosk just over the cleared border swath. Crossing in the dark is fine but climbers' trail above is hard to follow without daylight.
- **Fixed-rope waterfall scramble in Depot Creek:** the one objective hazard on the trail itself. Slippery wet rocks; serious slip risk with heavy pack.
- **Lake Ouzel basecamp** is the standard. The Eric/Steven shortcut to camp directly on the Redoubt Glacier (skipping Ouzel) is the right move only if you're trying to bag multiple peaks fast.

## Verbatim-worthy navigation hints (Spickard summit-day, from Lake Ouzel)

> "We ascended to just below the col leading to Silver Lake, then cut to the right. Luckily for us a group had just summited Spickard the previous day, so we could follow their tracks in the low visibility. We ascended up steep snow slopes to the south ridge of Spickard, then crossed a small notch to gain the east face snowfield. We traversed on the snowfield for a few hundred feet before finding a gully that we climbed back up to the south ridge. From there we roughly followed the ridge, traversing below a few false summits, until we scrambled up to the true summit in the early afternoon."

## Source confidence
- **Author reliability:** very high (Eric Gilbertson — well-known peakbagger, technically careful, trip reports consistently match other sources)
- **Route-status verification:** standard summer route as climbed in 2018; nothing in the description suggests it would have meaningfully changed since (the route is on permanent snow + rock features, not glacier-edge slabs subject to retreat)
