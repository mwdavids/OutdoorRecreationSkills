# Bonanza Peak (9,511 ft, Bulger #6)

## Summary
The highest non-volcanic peak in Washington and widely considered one of the **hardest Bulgers** — not for its technical grade but for its access. There is no road within day-trip distance of any standard route; every approach involves either the Lake Chelan ferry to Lucerne/Holden (boat + bus + foot) or a long traverse through the Glacier Peak Wilderness. The standard route (Mary Green Glacier) is moderate snow + mid-5th-class rock, but the logistics make a casual attempt impossible.

## Best-season window
- **Standard route (Mary Green Glacier from Holden), mid-July through early September:** glacier well consolidated but bridges still in, rock dry. The JSON `best_seasons: ["summer"]` is correct.
- **Spring ski mountaineering (Mary Green / Isella), May–early June:** viable but rare due to the access penalty — Holden Village road from Lucerne typically opens to vehicles by late spring; before that you walk it.
- **Late September / early October:** larches in Railroad Creek can be excellent, but glacier crevasses are open and snow bridges are gone — climbing gets harder, not easier.
- **Avoid:** mid-summer 2017+ era — Holden was repeatedly impacted by wildfire closures (Wolverine, Downey Creek). Check current InciWeb / NF status.

## Standard route — Mary Green Glacier from Holden Village
Lake Chelan ferry (Lady of the Lake from Chelan or Field's Point) → Lucerne dock → Holden Village bus (~11 mi) → hike up Railroad Creek to Holden Lake → ascend to Holden Pass → onto the **Mary Green Glacier** (visible large crevasses, rope up) → exit glacier onto rock slabs at its head → mid-5th-class rock to summit.

- **Stats:** ~3 days realistic. Day 1: travel + Holden + camp at Holden Lake or Holden Pass. Day 2: summit. Day 3: descend + exit ferry (ferry schedule is the binding constraint).
- **Grade:** Glacier travel + ~5.5 to 5.7 rock at the top, depending on variation
- **Crux on descent:** Mary Green Glacier exits onto rock slabs that get **streaming with snowmelt by mid-afternoon** — the route up is passable but the descent in wet conditions is dicey and parties often rappel. Eric Gilbertson rapped here on 2018-07-18; a **60m rope is needed for a clean rap** (40m falls short — see [reference TR](bonanza/references/2018-07-18-eric-gilbertson-dark-bonanza-traverse.md)).

## Alternate route — Dark-Bonanza traverse from Stehekin (Eric & Duncan, 2018)
Chelan ferry → Stehekin → NPS bus to High Bridge → south on PCT 12.5 mi to Hemlock Camp → leave PCT and ascend NW ridge of Dark Peak via **open old-growth forest (NOT Swamp Creek)** → bivy below Dark Glacier → Dark Peak summit → traverse ridge (4th & low 5th, one short ~5.6 step at the crux below Bonanza) → west peak → true summit → descend NE ridge then east face (class 3+) → Mary Green Glacier → Holden Pass → exit via Holden + Lake Chelan ferry.

- **One-way traverse** — exit logistics non-trivial (you started in Stehekin, exit via Lucerne)
- Blake Herrington lists this in his guidebook; it has been climbed only ~3 times Dark→Bonanza
- **Key beta correction:** Most guidebooks still list Swamp Creek as the Dark Peak approach. **Don't.** It's overgrown alder + vine maple. Use Hemlock Camp + NW ridge per Jake Robinson's beta — "more like open forest walking" (Eric, 2018-07-18).
- Detail: [Eric Gilbertson TR cache](bonanza/references/2018-07-18-eric-gilbertson-dark-bonanza-traverse.md)

## Access & logistics
- **Trailhead:** Lucerne dock (for Holden Village / standard route) or High Bridge (Stehekin, for Dark-Bonanza traverse)
- **Lake Chelan ferry:** Lady of the Lake operates from Chelan and Field's Point; **schedule is the binding constraint** for trip length. Options: Lady II (slow, ~4 hrs to Stehekin), Lady Express (fast, ~2.5 hrs). Reservations strongly advised in summer.
- **Holden Village bus:** runs in coordination with the ferry; book through Holden Village in advance. ~11 mi gravel road from Lucerne.
- **Drive from North Bend:** ~3.5 hrs to Chelan / Field's Point dock
- **Permits:** Wenatchee NF / Glacier Peak Wilderness (free wilderness self-issue at TH)
- **SNOTEL (per JSON):** `606:WA:SNTL` (Park Creek Ridge, 4,650 ft) — same SNOTEL serves Fernow & nearby Phelps Creek peaks; correlation with Bonanza-specific snowpack is loose at the Mary Green Glacier
- **NWAC zone (per JSON):** Cascade East Central — but this is a deep-interior peak; NWAC coverage will be coarse, prefer direct snowpack/glacier observations near trip date

## Crux / hazards

**Access is the crux.** A weather window that closes mid-trip leaves you ferry-locked in Lucerne or Stehekin for an extra day(s). Build flex into the schedule.

**Mary Green Glacier exit slabs (descent):** wet, mossy slabs streaming with afternoon snowmelt. Either descend early in the day or plan to rappel — **bring a 60m rope** (verified via Eric 2018-07-18, where a 40m did not reach).

**Mary Green Glacier crevasses:** "visible large crevasses" mid-July (Eric). Rope team mandatory. Bridges thin by late August.

**Summit rock:** mid-5th class, route-finding can be ambiguous. Unbelayed parties have been benighted descending — start the climbing pitches with enough daylight margin.

**Wildfire smoke / closures:** Holden basin and Lucerne have been repeatedly impacted (Wolverine 2015, more recent fires). Check InciWeb + Wenatchee NF closures within 2 weeks of trip.
