# Cosho Peak (8,332 ft, Bulger #94)

## Personal status
- **Climbed:** no
- **Style preference:** Likely best done as the **western terminus of the Ragged Ridge Traverse** with Kimtah on the same day from a 6,600-ft camp south of Mesahchie. See [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md), [katsuk.md](./katsuk.md), [kimtah.md](./kimtah.md). Westernmost and lowest of the four Bulgers on the ridge — has the best rock and is the most readily ski-able summit on a spring traverse.

## Summary
Westernmost summit on Ragged Ridge and the lowest of the four Ragged Ridge Bulgers. PeakBagger lidar: **8,338 ft** (8,332 ft in JSON, well within agreement). Prominence per LIDAR: **517 ft** (key col = Cosho-Kimtah pass 7,820 ft). Line parent: Kimtah Peak. Coords 48.5889 N, 120.9278 W. PeakBagger PID 1819.

Despite being the smallest summit on the ridge, **Cosho has the best rock quality of any of the four** — Kegan Buchhop (2023 FKT tie) describes the 7,900-ft col → summit scramble as "the most enjoyable in my opinion, it had the best quality rock on the whole ridge." Also the **only one of the four that's a credible ski summit** on a spring north-side traverse — Galen Weld and party skied to the saddle below Cosho and walked the last few hundred feet to the summit in late April 2025.

**Region 6: Ragged Ridge.** North Cascades NP, Stephen Mather Wilderness, Skagit County.

## Best-season window
- **Late July through mid-September** — prime. Cleanest rock on the ridge, sunrise/sunset panoramas from the summit toward Goode, Logan, Buckner, Sahale, Boston, and Forbidden.
- **June – mid-July:** snow on the "glacier" north of Thieves Peak (between Kimtah and Cosho) may require roping, though Lemke saw no cracks staying near the edge. The class 3-4 final 250 ft to summit is best after July melt-out.
- **Late April – early May:** **ski variant is in.** Easiest of the four to ski-tag; saddle below summit is a natural ski camp.

## Access — Easy Pass TH (SR 20)
Same as the other Ragged Ridge peaks. See [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md) for details.

## Standard route — East Ridge from Kimtah col (Class 3–4)
The classic line. From the standard traverse, Cosho is reached by continuing west past Kimtah's summit, descending to the Kimtah-Cosho col, then crossing a small snowfield north of "Thieves Peak" to the upper east face of Cosho.

### Approach
1. Easy Pass TH → Easy Pass → 6,600-ft basin camp south of Mesahchie (~5 h). See [mesahchie.md](./mesahchie.md).
2. **From camp, ascend to the 7,400-ft bench** (Class 4 on the final 250 ft).
3. **Traverse west on the 7,400-ft contour**, bypass the **Grotesque Gendarmes** (250–300 ft Class 4 descent crux — see [kimtah.md](./kimtah.md)).
4. **Ascend Kimtah** via its south-face gully (Class 3, "nature's staircase" on solid granite).
5. **From Kimtah summit, head west** traversing just south of the main sub-summit. **Cairns appear at this point** (first cairns of the day, per Lemke).
6. Route stays close to the **ridge crest** the whole way, following **ledges and gullies just south of the crest**.
7. Reach the **col between "Thieves Peak" and Kimtah quickly.**

### Cosho summit pitch
8. **Jump onto the "glacier"** (small snowfield) and **traverse north of "Thieves Peak."**
9. Lemke: **"no reason to rope up as we stayed close to the edge of the snowfield and saw no cracks."** This may vary year to year — bring rope + glacier gear if approaching in early season or low-coverage years.
10. **Ascend the snow until reaching the upper east face of Cosho.**
11. **Final 250 ft: Class 3-4 scramble on excellent rock** to the summit.
12. Lemke (2014) found an empty aluminum can but no register at the summit.

### Descent
**Reverse the route to the Cosho-Kimtah col.** Instead of re-climbing Kimtah, **traverse lower on the south side following cairns** on a ledge system that drops back to the standard ascent gully on Kimtah. Reverse the Gendarmes bypass and return to camp at sunset.

### Stats
- **Kimtah + Cosho from camp:** ~11–12 h on the move with photo stops.
- **From Easy Pass TH (c2c, Cosho only):** awkward — you have to do the whole Kimtah traverse first, so Cosho-only c2c is essentially the same as Kimtah+Cosho c2c. ~20 mi RT, ~7,500 ft gain.

### Alternative descent (FKT speed): the Cosho gully
- **The descent gully directly off Cosho south to the trail** is **NOT recommended** per Kegan Buchhop (2023 FKT comment):
  - "Terribly loose rock, 4th class downclimbs over dry waterfalls, loose vegetated terrain."
  - "Getting from the gully back to the trail involved ~¼ mile of thick alder bushwhack that took 45 mins."
- Use only if you're racing the clock and the Gendarmes bypass return route looks worse.

## Equipment
- **Helmet** — loose rock on the snowfield traverse and especially on the Cosho descent gully if used.
- **30-m rope + a couple cams** — useful for the Class 4 sections on the summit pitch, and as a glacier rope if the Thieves Peak snowfield looks crevassed in early season.
- **Light glacier gear** (1 picket, harness, 1-2 prussiks) for spring/early-summer if any concern about the snowfield.
- Approach shoes + ice axe + light crampons through August.

## Ski variant — primary ski summit on Ragged Ridge
Per Galen Weld 2025-05 partial ski traverse: **Cosho is the easy ski tag from the standard 3-glacier spring traverse.** The saddle on Ragged Ridge **just below the summit of Cosho** is "the saddle on Ragged Ridge just below the summit of Cosho Peak, the westernmost and most diminutive summit of the ridge, where we planned on camping." From this saddle, **drop overnight gear and head up the last few hundred feet to the summit on foot or skins.**

Standard 3-glacier ski traverse:
1. Easy Pass TH → Easy Pass (skinning above ~4,000 ft snowline).
2. **Mesahchie Glacier** (drop into Panther Creek).
3. **Katsuk Glacier** (climb to Katsuk-Pachyderm ridge).
4. **Kimtah Glacier** (climb above icefall, traverse to Cosho saddle).
5. **Camp at Cosho saddle.** Tag Cosho on foot.
6. **Exit:** reverse to Mesahchie Pass → ski Granite Creek drainage to SR 20 (car shuttle).

**Conditions window: roughly the week SR 20 reopens through ~mid-May**, snow-dependent. Bushwhack ~30 min from Easy Pass TH to continuous snow in lighter snow years.

## Hazards
- **Snowfield north of Thieves Peak:** crevasse risk varies by year/season. Stay near edge in late season; rope up in early season or low-coverage years.
- **Cosho descent gully** (south to trail): loose 4th class + ¼ mi alder bushwhack. Avoid unless racing.
- **Long return through Gendarmes bypass with tired legs at sunset.**
