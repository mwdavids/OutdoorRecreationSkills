# Flora Mountain

**Bulger #99 · 8320 ft · Prom 1800 ft**

## Status
Unclimbed (as of May 2026).

## Personal Context
- Home base and style context: North Bend, generally prefers car-to-car day trips where feasible.
- Flora is typically a logistics-heavy objective because standard access depends on Lake Chelan boat + Stehekin transport.
- No known prior Flora attempt logged yet.

## Summary
Flora Mountain is Bulger #99 and is usually climbed from Stehekin via the Devore Creek Trail system. The mountain can be paired with nearby Devore and Tupshin for Bulger-focused multi-day trips, often from a camp in the Bird Creek drainage.

The standard southwest-side line is mostly non-technical hiking/scrambling terrain when melted out, but the overall effort is substantial due to long approach logistics and sustained trail climbing. A more complex Holden/Tenmile carryover variation exists and involves harder routefinding and short Class 3/4 sections.

## Best-Season Window
- Primary window: mid-summer through early fall (roughly July to September) when high-route terrain is mostly snow-free.
- Early season: expect lingering snow in passes and traverses, which can increase complexity and gear requirements.
- Shoulder season weather can quickly degrade long exposed traverses; prioritize high-confidence forecast windows.

Candidate seasons: summer

## Standard Route
Recommended standard: Stehekin Landing -> Devore Creek Trail -> Bird Creek Camp staging -> southwest parkland basin -> talus finish to summit.

Typical effort profile: multi-day outing due to access and approach overhead, with Flora commonly climbed as a long day from Bird Creek Camp.

### Approach
Stehekin/Devore approach (preferred "easy" route vs Holden-side route):
- Reach Stehekin via Lake Chelan ferry.
- From Stehekin Landing, take shuttle (or bike/walk/ride) about 4.4 mi to Harlequin Bridge.
- Cross bridge, continue past Harlequin Camp road to wye, go left, pass maintenance yard and Stehekin airfield.
- Follow track to Stehekin River Trail and continue about 3 mi to signed Devore Creek Trail junction (~1160 ft) near Weaver Point spur.

Devore Creek Trail climb:
- Initial 2 mi gain via many switchbacks to near wilderness boundary (~2300 ft).
- Then another 3+ mi of sustained uphill; middle section can be brushy and slower.
- Reach Bird Creek area around 4200 ft; designated Bird Creek Camp is just before creek crossing (space for about 3 tents + cook area).

Planning note:
- From Harlequin Bridge, approximately 4 hours to Bird Creek Camp for many parties.
- Bird Creek Camp is a common and efficient staging camp for Flora; no strong need to push farther unless carrying through.

### Summit Route
From Bird Creek Camp / upper Devore Creek Trail:
- Continue toward upper basin terrain and trend into the broad southwest-side parkland basin below Flora.
- Work through mixed talus and alpine benches on the southwest side.
- Finish via straightforward talus/scree hiking to the summit.

Difficulty:
- Primarily hiking with easy scramble elements in dry conditions (commonly treated as non-technical or easy Class 2/3 terrain depending exact line).

Main challenge is endurance and route efficiency over long approach terrain, not technical climbing.

### Descent
Normally reverse the ascent route back to Bird Creek Camp and then down Devore Creek Trail to Stehekin.

If doing carryover logistics, descent options may continue toward Holden-side trail systems, but this is a separate itinerary with added complexity.

## Equipment
- Summer standard kit: helmet optional but recommended in loose talus terrain, poles, sturdy footwear, navigation tools, and full day emergency layer setup.
- Shoulder season additions: ice axe and traction if snow remains on high traverses or pass features.
- Rope/protection: generally not needed on standard Stehekin/Devore line in dry summer conditions.
- Brush management on approach: lightweight long sleeves/pants useful for brushy middle section of Devore Creek Trail.

## Alternates / Variations
1. Holden/Tenmile carryover variation (longer and more complex):
	- Lucerne -> Holden bus logistics -> Tenmile Creek approach -> Tenmile Pass (~6500 ft).
	- Descend into Devore drainage, then traverse via Riddle Peaks saddle and ridge/divide terrain toward Flora.
	- Contains routefinding complexity and short Class 3/4 terrain around Pt. 7990 / traverse features.
2. Stehekin floatplane optimization:
	- Drop at Weaver Point can reduce flat valley walking if arranged and group size requirements are met.
3. Multi-peak linking:
	- Flora can be combined with Devore and Tupshin from a shared Bird Creek staging camp.

## Access Notes & Permits
- Primary access is by Lake Chelan boat system (ferry) to Stehekin.
- Stehekin local transport often uses shuttle service between landing and trail access points.
- Devore Creek Trail has long sustained climbing and can be brushy; pace planning matters.
- Bird Creek Camp is the only designated camp in that immediate section near the creek crossing.
- Validate current ferry schedules, shuttle operations, wilderness permit rules, and trail advisories before committing.

## Tactical Notes
- Heat management matters on lower Devore Creek Trail; late-afternoon ascent to camp can be more comfortable than midday.
- Expect a grind: sustained elevation gain over long distance with limited relief.
- Bird Creek Camp is a strong tactical base for Flora and nearby objectives.
- One full day per summit is the conservative planning baseline; very fast parties may combine Devore + Tupshin in one day.
- Hazards: brush slowdown, dehydration/heat on lower trail, loose talus near summit terrain, and navigation inefficiency in poor visibility.

## Combinations
- Classic Bulger combo zone: Flora + Devore + Tupshin from Bird Creek Camp or nearby Devore staging.
- Carryover itinerary potential: Devore-side to Holden-side via high route variants.
- For efficiency-focused teams, Flora can be sequenced with adjacent objectives over multiple camp-based days.
