# Eric Gilbertson + Francis — Buckner, Horseshoe, Sahale Winter Ascents (Feb 3-4, 2024)

**Source:** https://www.countryhighpoints.com/buckner-horseshoe-sahale-winter-ascents/
**Author:** Eric Gilbertson
**Posted:** 2024-02-07
**Trip dates:** 2024-02-03 to 2024-02-04
**Party:** Eric Gilbertson, Francis
**Outcome:** Buckner summit (winter), Horseshoe summit (First Winter Ascent, SE Face M3), Sahale summit (winter). All three from a single Sahale Camp basecamp.

## Why preserved in full
**Documents a Buckner route that completely bypasses both the Sahale-Boston Col and the Boston Glacier** — the SW face from Horseshoe Basin. This is the route that solves the problem we hit on 2026-05-04. Also documents Cascade River Road winter access logistics and Sahale Arm / Sahale Camp as a winter basecamp for multi-peak days in this group.

## Day-by-day

### Friday Feb 2 — Cascade River Road access
- Drove up Cascade River Rd Friday evening, **logged out 6 trees up to a foot diameter** (25" chainsaw)
- **NOHRSC model wildly wrong:** predicted deep snow at MP 18; road was actually snow-free all the way to the Eldorado gate at MP 20 and beyond
- Slept at Eldorado gate
- Boston Basin trailhead (MP 22) road section above the gate has a **huge washout at Boston Creek — Eric expects it to stay gated well into summer for road repairs**
- Eric's prior winter recon (mid-Jan): drove to MP 15, snowmobiled from there to Eldo gate sawing trees en route. Snowmobile rule of thumb: not more than ~½ mi on gravel with two people + gear (overheats).

### Saturday Feb 3 — Horseshoe + Buckner from Sahale Camp
- Up at 1am. Skinned/walked road snow-free to Boston Basin TH at 3,200 ft. Snow started just after Soldier Boy Creek; switched to skis.
- Skinned Cascade Pass trail to last switchback before the traverse to the pass; cramponed up few hundred feet through open trees, then skinned again angling up + left around a cliff band; popped onto **Sahale Arm at sunrise** (Johannesburg dramatic across valley above undercast).
- Continued skinning to **Sahale Camp**. Snow softer with elevation.
- **Decision: brought overnight gear DOWN to Horseshoe Basin** instead of leaving it at Sahale Camp — gives flexibility if climbs run long; avoids forced 1,000 ft re-climb back to camp at end of day.
- **Decision: skied SE bowl** down from Sahale Camp to Horseshoe Basin instead of downclimbing the SE ridge (4th class step would be sketchy with big packs + skis).
- Route through SE bowl: shaded relief showed a route avoiding cliffs; **Tim Gibson posted video on TurnsAllYear of skiing this route in May** — confirmed feasible with snow coverage. Timed departure to hit this section after sunrise to assess before committing.
- Skied bowl with leap-frog turns; powder on crust; cut down hard skiers' right through cliff-band weakness; reached basin floor at **6,500 ft**.

**Horseshoe (First Winter Ascent, SE Face, M3):**
- Stashed overnight gear at flat sheltered area at base of SE ridge
- Skinned across basin (lots of avalanche debris from prior week's atmospheric river); through old mining site; zigzagged up to bench at **8,200 ft** below Horseshoe; ditched skis, crampons on
- Kicked steps up snow, scrambled over small ridge to gully at base of SE face
- Up gully, ditched gear at large notch east of summit
- **Crux: ramp up + left to second notch at summit.** A foot of rime ice plastering rocks. One pitch (40-50 ft):
  - Anchor: horn at notch, sling rapped around it
  - Belay: Eric led; trouble finding gear under rime; gold-mined cleaning rime to find cracks; cracks all icy — wanted to pound hexes but horizontal cracks under overhang prevented it; ended up using cams
  - Careful footwork — couldn't kick all snow off ledge but couldn't trust thin slab either; got tools sunk to good hooks before trusting feet
  - Final move: hooked good jug with tool, pulled over to clip rap anchor at the second notch
- Tagged summit; belayed Francis up by 2pm
- **Rappel: 60m rope was right call — comfortably reached gully below; 30m would not have reached.**

**Buckner via SW face from Horseshoe Basin:**
- Back at skis. Plenty of time to add Buckner before sunset.
- Slope icy → strapped skis to packs and traversed over to **SW face of Buckner**
- Brought ascent plates but only sank ankle-deep — not needed
- **A few isolated 6" wind slab pockets** — stuck to edge of face climbing between flattish areas below cliffs to minimize exposure
- Took turns kicking steps to **SE ridge**; ditched packs/skis
- Scrambled up thickly rime-encrusted summit block; **summit at 4:30pm**
- Eric peered down N face, "happy not to be skiing that" — risky with wind slabs
- Survey background: Eric's 2022 theodolite work confirmed SW summit is 18" ± 3" taller than NE; saved time not bothering with NE peak
- Downclimbed to skis; skied gully hugging up tracks; back to base of Horseshoe; collected climbing gear; skied/skinned back to overnight gear by 6pm at sunset

### Saturday night — re-ascent to Sahale Camp via ascent plates
- Site at 6,500 ft was too windy for camping
- Slope back up the bowl: too steep to skin, too deep to boot
- **Solution: ascent plates** — mini snowshoes for steep snow faces. Eric has aluminum + ultralight carbon-fiber pair he made.
- Francis led with aluminum pair; Eric followed; "much faster progress than bare booting"
- Popped onto Sahale Camp shoulder by 8pm
- Set up megamid in semi-sheltered spot; melted snow; **19-hour day**

### Sunday Feb 4 — Sahale + ski out
- Slept in; moving by 8:30am for warmer climb
- Roped up for Sahale Glacier; zigzagged up; on SE ridge packed rope, ditched skis, cramponed up icy slope
- Two routes considered to summit:
  - South face direct (5th class step, normally summer route — but icy/rimed bad odds)
  - East face traverse → north ridge (3rd class but very exposed, 2,000 ft drop to Davenport Glacier)
- Chose east-face traverse (didn't want to risk rime over 5th class). Roped up — even easy snow climb, didn't want a wind slab knocking them off
- Built anchor in rock outcrop on SE ridge; led across face traversing onto east aspect; gear in rock outcrop; kicked steps straight up hugging cliff band; rime cleaning to find cracks for gear
- Popped onto N ridge; final moves to summit
- Found Boston Peak USGS marker on summit; rap anchor around summit boulder
- Belayed Francis up; views of Forbidden, Eldorado, Boston (briefly out of clouds); 11:45am
- **Rappel: 60m just barely reached lower anchor.** Again Eric was glad he hadn't brought 30m.
- Cramponed to skis; **continuous ski run 8,500 → 5,000 ft**; bypassed lower 5 switchbacks via open snow slope shortcut; back to road, skied to stashed boots near Soldier Boy Creek; hiked to Eldo gate by 3:45pm

## Key Buckner-specific takeaways

- **The SW face from Horseshoe Basin completely bypasses the Sahale-Boston Col / Boston Glacier problem.** Standard winter line for Eric's group; should also work as a spring/early-season alternate to the N Face for parties not committed to skiing the N Face proper.
- **Approach:** Eldorado TH → Cascade Pass → Sahale Arm → Sahale Camp → ski SE bowl down to Horseshoe Basin (~6,500 ft) → traverse to SW face → SE ridge → summit.
- **SE bowl ski-down from Sahale Camp:** route through cliff bands hard skiers' right; needs snow coverage. Tim Gibson TurnsAllYear video shows it skiable in May.
- **SW face climb:** moderate steep snow with isolated wind-slab pockets in winter. Easier in conditions where slabs aren't an issue.
- **N Face descent option (skiing):** Eric "happy not to be skiing that" in Feb 2024 conditions — wind slab risk. The N Face *ski* line still requires the col approach (or topping out via SW and skiing the N then climbing back up). Trade-off: you climb SW for a safer summit but lose the N Face ski.
- **NE summit not needed:** SW summit is 18" ±3" taller (Eric 2022 theodolite survey). Skip NE traverse.

## Key access takeaways

- **Cascade River Road winter access:** highly variable, NOHRSC model unreliable. Possible to drive to Eldo gate (MP 20) in low-snow winters; otherwise snowmobile from MP 15ish. Bring 25" chainsaw — multiple foot-diameter blowdowns expected per winter.
- **Boston Basin TH (MP 22) road section:** Boston Creek washout means it stays gated well into summer for repairs. Add 2 mi walk/bike for Boston Basin approach.
- **2023 enforcement note:** USFS law enforcement turned Eric + Ryan back at MP 13 in winter 2023 with no clear reason given. Worth knowing it's possible to get stopped.
