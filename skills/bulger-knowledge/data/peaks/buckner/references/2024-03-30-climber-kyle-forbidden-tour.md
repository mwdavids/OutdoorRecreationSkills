## Trip summary
- **Date:** March 30, 2024
- **Party:** 4
- **Snowpack year:** ~60% of normal (low)
- **Route:** Eldorado TH (gated) → Boston Basin Trail → Boston Basin → Sharkfin Col → Boston Glacier descent → ascent to col on Forbidden N Ridge → ski to Moraine Lake → exit via Eldorado approach
- **Stats:** 18 mi, 10,000 ft gain, 13.5 hrs

## Beta relevant to Buckner via Sharkfin Col

### Sharkfin Col itself
- Right-side snow couloir to a notch ~20–30 ft above the col proper, fixed slings for rappel anchor
- Left-side rock variant: ~5.7 / loose Class 3-4 gully, exposed; Kyle bailed off it when it had alpine ice in the narrow runnel
- Single 30m rope = one rappel + some downclimbing; 2 ropes tied or one 60m = clean full rappel
- Small cornice on col may need to be broken through to start rappel

### Boston Glacier
- **Well filled in late March** — easy travel for Kyle's party
- Large and complex; rope team mandatory
- Opens up significantly in June

### Boston Basin Trail approach
- Required alder shuttling in Kyle's low-snow year
- Would be cleaner in average / above-average year, ideally late April with cold-night-warm-day pattern

## How to adapt for Buckner-only
Reverse the Forbidden Tour middle: from Sharkfin Col, ski **east** across Boston Glacier to the base of Buckner's N Face, climb/ski the face, return back across the glacier, exit via the col rappel/booter.

Realistically a 2-day trip with bivy in Boston Basin (or on the glacier). A fast strong party could do c2c in 16–18+ hrs.

## Caveats
- Low-snow year — alder problem on Boston Basin Trail may be worse than typical
- Forbidden Tour party did not climb Buckner; the eastward leg across Boston Glacier is inferred, not observed in this TR
- 2024-03-30 conditions are a data point, not a forecast — verify against current snowpack before relying on this beta
