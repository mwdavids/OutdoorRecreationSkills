## Attempts & Turnarounds

### Mount Buckner — 2026-05-04 — TURNED AROUND
- **Plan:** N Face ski mountaineering, c2c day trip from Eldorado TH (Cascade River Rd gate ~MP 21), bike approach
- **Result:** Turned around at the Sahale-Boston Col
- **Reason:** Traverse from Sahale-Boston Col onto upper Boston Glacier was extremely steep snow — impassable in early-May conditions
- **Lesson:** Sahale-Boston Col → Boston Glacier traverse is the make-or-break section in May/early June, not the N Face itself. See `peaks/buckner.md` for full beta and the Sharkfin Col alternate.
