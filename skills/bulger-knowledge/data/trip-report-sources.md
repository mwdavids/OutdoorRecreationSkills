# Trip Report Sources for Bulger Peaks

Where to look for current and historical trip reports, ranked by typical signal-to-noise for technical Cascade alpine. Use this to ground briefings in real beta rather than guessing search results.

## General-purpose, all peaks

- **CascadeClimbers Forums** — `https://cascadeclimbers.com/forum/` — Long-form alpine TRs from PNW climbers. Best signal for technical climbs and ski mountaineering. Search by peak name.
- **NWHikers TR Database** — `https://www.nwhikers.net/forums/viewforum.php?f=3` — Hiking-focused but useful for non-technical Bulgers and approach beta.
- **WTA (Washington Trails Association)** — `https://www.wta.org/go-outside/trip-reports` — Trail/approach conditions; weak on technical summits but excellent for road, gate, and snow-line observations.
- **SummitPost** — `https://www.summitpost.org/` — Older but durable route descriptions; thin on recent conditions.
- **Mountain Project** — `https://www.mountainproject.com/` — Strong for rock routes (Stuart, Dragontail, Forbidden W Ridge, etc.); weak for ski/glacier objectives.

## Ski mountaineering & spring conditions

- **TurnsAllYear (TAY)** — `https://www.turns-all-year.com/skiing_the_mountains/trip_reports/` — *The* PNW ski-mountaineering forum. Essential for spring beta on volcano and alpine descents.

## Official sources (conditions, permits, closures)

- **NOCA Climbing Information (NPS)** — `https://www.nps.gov/noca/planyourvisit/climbing.htm` — North Cascades National Park current conditions, permit zones, ranger reports.
- **MRNP Climbing** — `https://www.nps.gov/mora/planyourvisit/climbing.htm` — Rainier ranger blog and route conditions.
- **Mt Baker-Snoqualmie NF Alerts** — `https://www.fs.usda.gov/mbs` — Road and trail status.
- **Okanogan-Wenatchee NF Alerts** — `https://www.fs.usda.gov/okawen` — Methow and east-side road status.
- **WSDOT Mountain Pass Reports** — `https://wsdot.com/travel/real-time/mountainpasses` — SR 20 opening dates etc.

## Personal blogs & repeat sources (Cascade alpinists worth bookmarking)

> Add specific blog/Instagram/Strava handles here as you find authors who consistently produce useful beta. Don't assume URLs — verify first.

- **Country High Points** - `https://www.countryhighpoints.com/washington-hundred-highest-bulgers/` - One or more trip report for each bulger. 
- **Hike 2 Hike** - `https://hike2hike.com/bulger-peaks/` - Trip reports for many bulgers.
- **Steph Abegg** — `http://www.stephabegg.com/home/tripreports` — Detailed photo-rich TRs across NW alpine.
- **Northwest Mountaineering Journal** (NWMJ) — `http://www.mountaineers.org/about/history/the-nw-mountaineering-journal-archives` — Annual journal, excellent for FAs and uncommon objectives.
- **Climber Kyle** - `https://climberkyle.com/` -- Trip reports all over the cascades. Not Bulger focused, but often approaches or climbs Bulger peaks as part of traverses or high routes.

## Per-region cheat-sheet

| Region | Best forum | Typical TR style |
|---|---|---|
| Stuart Range | CascadeClimbers, Mountain Project | Rock + alpine |
| N Cascades (Skagit, Eldorado, Boston, Forbidden) | CascadeClimbers, TAY | Glacier, mixed, ski mtn |
| Methow / Pasayten | NWHikers, CascadeClimbers | Long approach scrambles |
| Stehekin / Bonanza / Holden area | CascadeClimbers | Multi-day alpine |
| Chiwawa / Entiat | CascadeClimbers, NWHikers | Scramble + ski mtn |
| Volcanoes (Rainier, Baker, Adams, Glacier) | TAY, CascadeClimbers | Ski mtn + glacier |

## Search-query patterns that work

When searching forums, prefer:
- `"{peak name}" {year}` (e.g., `"Mount Buckner" 2025`) for recency
- `"{peak name}" {route or feature}` (e.g., `"Buckner" "Sharkfin Col"`) for route-specific beta
- `"{peak name}" {season}` (e.g., `"Bonanza" "May"`) for seasonal conditions

Avoid generic Google searches that surface AllTrails / SummitPost stubs ahead of real TRs.
