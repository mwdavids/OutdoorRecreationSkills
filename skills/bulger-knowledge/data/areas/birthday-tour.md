# Birthday Tour (Washington Pass)

## What it is
Classic spring ski tour on the **east side of Washington Pass / North Cascades Highway (SR 20)**, running between **Cutthroat Peak** (8,050') and **Whistler Mountain** (7,790'). It is a **non-glaciated ski mountaineering tour** — alpine bowls, couloirs, and ridge cols on volcanic-and-granite terrain. There are no permanent glaciers or crevasse hazards on the standard line.

## Standard line
- Start at the **Blue Lake trailhead** on SR 20 (~MP 161, ~5,200 ft) once the highway is open.
- Climb toward Blue Lake / Cutthroat-Whistler col area, then link bowls and short couloirs across the south flanks of Cutthroat and over to Whistler.
- ~6–10 mi RT, ~3,500–4,500 ft gain depending on variation, mostly south- and east-facing aspects.
- Generally a **day tour** for a fit party. Not a summit-bagging objective — the "tour" is the point, not tagging Cutthroat or Whistler proper.

## Season
- **Late April through May** is the sweet spot: SR 20 is typically reopened (late April most years), corn cycle is on, snowpack still continuous from the highway.
- **Too early (March / pre-SR-20-opening):** access is the problem, not the snow. SR 20 closes ~late November and reopens late April / early May. Pre-opening you're skinning miles of flat highway from the Silver Star gate.
- **Too late (mid-June onward):** lower bowls melt out, approach gets walky/brushy, refreeze unreliable.

## Hazards
- **Avalanche** on the steep south/east bowls — under NWAC East North zone forecast. Wet-loose and wet-slab in spring corn cycles; persistent slab earlier in season.
- **Cornice** along the Cutthroat-Whistler ridgeline.
- **Highway re-closures** for spring storms — SR 20 can re-close in May.
- No glacier travel, no bergschrunds, no technical climbing on the standard tour.

## Disambiguation
- "Birthday Tour" in the PNW context = **Washington Pass / Cutthroat-Whistler ski tour** (~48.52, -120.66).
- Not to be confused with: any peak named "Birthday", or unrelated ski tours called "birthday" elsewhere (Wasatch, etc.).
- Standard write-ups: Martin Volken's *Backcountry Skiing: Snoqualmie Pass* and *Cascade Ski Tours*; TAY/CC.com trip reports under "Birthday Tour" / "Cutthroat Whistler".

## What this tour is NOT
- Not glaciated.
- Not a summit climb of Cutthroat Peak (that's a separate W Ridge / SW Couloir objective).
- Not a multi-day traverse.
- Not appropriate before SR 20 reopens.
