# Seasonal crux — when to emit the section

The `## Seasonal Crux` section is **conditional**, not mandatory. Include
it ONLY when there is a real make-or-break section of the trip driven by
the time of year. If no seasonal crux applies (e.g. dry summer scramble in
normal conditions), **omit the section entirely** — do not emit an H2 with
empty content.

## What counts as a seasonal crux

- A **col traverse** loaded with steep snow (e.g. Sahale-Boston Col in
  May/June).
- A **glacier bergschrund** opening up at the top of a snow climb (e.g.
  Nohokomeen Headwall on Jack Mtn, closes early-to-mid June).
- **River crossings** at peak melt (snowmelt-fed creeks in late May / early
  June can be impassable in afternoon, fine at dawn).
- **Road snow** blocking the trailhead approach (closure-side or
  unmaintained gate).
- A **brushed-in approach** (Boston Basin Trail in low-snow years has
  alder thrash sections that disappear under continuous snow coverage).
- A **cornice** that may or may not have dropped (south- and west-facing
  summit cornices in May).
- A **hanging serac** that's actively pulsing (rare to call as seasonal
  but counts if there's recent activity).

## Format

2-4 sentences naming three things:

1. **The feature** (specific — name the col, the schrund, the gully).
2. **Current likelihood it's in play** (use proxies — SNOTEL panel, recent
   storm sequence, the climber's own attempt history, trip reports).
3. **The bail criterion** (what observable thing means turn around).

## Examples

> **Sharkfin Col approach gully** — fully snow-filled and skiable through
> mid-May in average years; this year's 41% SWE at Lyman Lake suggests it
> may melt out 2-3 weeks earlier than the 2024 reference TR. Bail criterion:
> if the right-side snow couloir shows rock striping below the cornice on
> the approach, the standard rappel anchor won't reach snow — reverse to
> Boston Basin and try the rock variation only if your party has 5.7
> on-sight capacity with loose gear.

> **Nohokomeen Glacier bergschrund** — typical close-out window is early
> to mid-June. Eric Gilbertson June 2 2018 found one bridge with ~1 week
> of life left; this year is running ahead of season, so plan for a
> May-15 to May-25 window and expect the schrund to be open by Memorial
> Day. Bail criterion: no continuous snow ramp across the schrund visible
> from the upper Nohokomeen Glacier — turn around, do not attempt to end-run.

> **Cascade River Rd snow gate at MP 20** — Eldorado TH still has 2 mi of
> snow-covered road as of last report. Bring a fat-tire bike for the
> approach; if the road is rideable now, the Boston Basin Trail above
> should have continuous snow coverage and no alder.

## When NOT to include

- Midsummer dry-trail scrambles in normal conditions.
- Fall hikes on fully melted-out terrain.
- Any trip where the route is in its plateau season (e.g. Stuart via
  Cascadian Couloir in mid-July) and there's no time-of-year feature on
  the line.

If you can't name a specific feature with a specific bail criterion, you
don't have a seasonal crux. Skip the section.
